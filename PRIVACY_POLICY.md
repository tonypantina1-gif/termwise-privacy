# TermTide Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 November 2026

TermTide is an iPhone app developed and operated by **Faton Pantina** ("we", "us"). It helps parents turn school letters, emails and messages into dates, reminders and checklists. It is built so that **we never collect, receive or store your data**. Everything you add stays on your iPhone.

This policy explains what that means in practice. If anything is unclear, contact us at **aafdigital@proton.me**.

---

## The short version

- **No data collected.** We do not collect any personal information, about you or your children.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Children's names, school letters, dates, items and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
- **No servers, no accounts.** TermTide has no backend and no sign-up. There is nothing for us to log into or look at.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no ads.** TermTide contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software, and no third-party code at all.
- **Payments are handled by Apple.** Subscriptions and purchases go through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

On the App Store, TermTide's privacy label is **"Data Not Collected"**.

---

## What TermTide stores, and where

TermTide stores the following **only on your iPhone**, inside the app's own storage:

- Your children's profiles: name, school (optional), colour and avatar icon.
- Items you confirm: titles, dates and times, amounts, notes, reminder settings, and the sentence from the original letter or message each one came from.
- Your app settings, for example whether you have finished the welcome screens.

The TermTide widget and Live Activity read this same on-device data so they can show your upcoming items. They do not send it anywhere.

### Letters, photos and documents

When you scan a letter, choose a photo or screenshot, paste a message, or share text, a link or a PDF to TermTide, the text is read **on your iPhone** using Apple's on-device text recognition. Images and documents are not uploaded. After reading:

- Scanned pages and photos are **not kept**. Only the items you confirm are saved.
- Items shared to TermTide from other apps are held on your device only until you review them, then deleted.

**Smart Triage:** on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, TermTide can use Apple's **on-device** language model to label found items (for example "Urgent Deadline" or "Kit Required"). This runs entirely on your iPhone; the text is not sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off in TermTide's Settings, and on other iPhones labels come from simple keyword rules instead.

TermTide does not download web pages. For a link shared from Safari, it reads only the page text that Safari passes to it on your device.

## Permissions TermTide may ask for

- **Camera:** to scan paper school letters. Images are processed on your device and not stored.
- **Notifications:** to remind you about items (for example at 6:00 PM the evening before) and, if you choose, a morning checklist. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; no notification passes through any server of ours.
- **Photos:** TermTide uses Apple's photo picker, so it only ever receives the specific photos you choose. It does not have access to your photo library.
- **Calendar:** "Add to Calendar" opens Apple's own event editor. You choose the calendar and confirm. TermTide cannot read your calendars.
- **Motion:** the gentle tilt effect on the empty dashboard uses your iPhone's motion sensors while that screen is visible. Motion data is not stored or sent anywhere.

You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

---

## When information leaves your iPhone (only when you choose)

TermTide never sends your information anywhere by itself. Information leaves your device only when **you** take one of these actions:

- **Add to Calendar:** the event goes into the calendar you pick. If that calendar syncs through iCloud or another account, that provider's privacy policy applies.
- **Share via Messages:** the item's details go to whoever you send them to.
- **Export backup:** TermTide creates a file containing your children and items. You decide where to save it (for example Files or iCloud Drive). We never receive it.
- **Send Feedback:** opens your own email app with a message to us. It includes your device model, iOS version and TermTide version so we can fix problems, but nothing about your children, letters or items. You can read and change the email before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

---

## Device backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple includes TermTide's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. This is controlled by your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access these backups.

---

## Payments

Purchases and subscriptions (TermTide Pro) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. TermTide checks whether you have Pro using Apple's StoreKit on your device. We do not receive your name, email address, payment card or Apple Account details. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions** on your iPhone.

---

## Information Apple may share with developers

TermTide includes no analytics or crash-reporting software. However, if you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to **share analytics with app developers**, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics about TermTide. Apple collects and anonymises this; it does not identify you, and you can turn it off in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements**.

If you take part in a TestFlight beta, feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send through TestFlight are shared with us by Apple under Apple's TestFlight terms.

---

## Children's privacy

TermTide is designed for parents and carers, not for children to use. Although you may enter your children's names and school details, this information stays on your device and is never collected by us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

---

## Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your iPhone, you are always in control of it:

- Delete an item or a child in the app (deleting a child also deletes their items).
- Deleting TermTide from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device.
- Any backup files you exported are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

There is no copy of your data held by us to request or delete.

---

## Changes to this policy

If we change how TermTide handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date. If a change ever meant data leaving your device in a new way, we would explain it in the app before it happened.
